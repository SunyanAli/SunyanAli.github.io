What actually happens

Someone calls your number

It answers on the first ring. Nine at night, Sunday morning, while you’re on a job. It doesn’t matter.

It asks what they need

Name, number, what the job is, when suits them. The same questions your receptionist would ask, in the same order.

It books them in

The appointment lands in your calendar with their details attached. You get a message telling you what was agreed.

If it can’t help, it hands over

Anything unusual, urgent, or outside what it knows goes to a person. It is built to stop rather than guess.

What it costs to run

The calls themselves cost about ten cents a minute. A booking call usually runs two or three minutes, so somewhere around a quarter per call.

That is what you pay the phone and software providers, not what you pay me. I would rather you see that number up front than find it in month two.
